FAQ

Questions we get before signing up.

Which review platforms do you support?

Google Business Profile and Trustpilot are supported at launch, submitted automatically once a review is approved under your review policy. Additional platforms can be scoped on request.

Are conversations handled by people or by a bot?

Trained human agents handle every conversation. A bot can triage and answer simple, repetitive questions if you want it, but it's always your call which parts stay human.

What happens if a customer leaves a negative review?

You set the policy: some businesses publish everything as-is, others route low ratings to a private follow-up first so the issue can be resolved before anything goes public. Nothing is filtered without your rules in place.

Can we customize the review request message and timing?

Yes — wording, timing after resolution, and which channels trigger a request are all configurable per business.

How fast can we get set up?

Most businesses are live on chat and email within a week; phone support and full review syndication typically follow in the second week once your review policy is confirmed.
